# Device frame sources and adaptations

The current preview and default are the original Macintosh. Other models remain optional prototypes. Source attribution does not imply endorsement by the photographers, museums, or Apple.

## Macintosh 128K — current default

`macintosh-128k-chassis.png` (1055 × 1490) is a built-in ImageGen reconstruction from the owner’s supplied Macintosh photograph, with its detached keyboard and mouse removed. It has alpha transparency, a black display placeholder, and a code-native silhouette clip. It is not an unchanged photograph or verified CAD model. Exact prompts: `docs/design/macintosh-128k-prototype.md`. The earlier museum-photo replacement was rejected and is not shipped.

## iMac G3 Blueberry — optional prototype

`imac-g3-cutout.png` (1254 × 1254) is a built-in ImageGen extraction/edit derived from Thomas Kaiser’s [IMac G3 blueberry front.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IMac_G3_blueberry_front.jpg), photographed 19 February 2007. The source describes a 2000 slot-loading Blueberry model. Adaptation: background removal, black replacement display, resampling, and alpha cleanup. It retains [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) and the credit “iMac G3 Blueberry photograph: Thomas Kaiser, CC BY-SA 3.0.”

Exact selected prompts and limitations: `imac-g3-cutout.md`. This prototype has not met the owner’s final product-photography standard. The later Snow study stays outside the public payload.

## iMac G4 — owner-supplied reference

`imac-g4-cutout.png` (1422 × 1106) is a built-in ImageGen edit of the owner’s front-facing G4 product photograph supplied on 6 November 2026. The speakers, keyboard, mouse, and cables were removed; the obscured bottom of the base was reconstructed and the display replaced with black. No source photographer or license was supplied with the attachment, so no public-domain or Creative Commons status is asserted. The source’s wide screen is preserved; exact SKU is unverified.

Prompts, source path, and geometry: `docs/design/imac-g4-cutout.md`.

## Interface adaptations

SVG silhouettes exclude extraction remnants, background, and detached peripherals. Native HTML replaces each display. Camera-fitting transforms and CRT/LCD glass are code-native. Retain credit and license links when distributing adaptations; share-alike continues to apply to the adapted G3 material.
