# Cohesive portfolio improvement plan

Goal: a visitor can understand Isaac’s strengths, investigate specific work and follow a line of inquiry without encountering irrelevant answers, repeated copy or broken interactions.

“Compelling” means a direct answer backed by a concrete example, a clear distinction between Isaac’s responsibilities and team results, and a useful next interaction. Acceptance requires evidence, rather than a promise of perfection.

1. Establish the current live baseline. Record public content version, actual routing/composition responses and representative screenshots. Test natural questions and follow-ups rather than relying on layout presets.
2. Evaluate answer quality. Cover career, public service, Taiwan, Congress, product feedback, journalism and comparisons. Grade subject, requested aspect, specific evidence, attribution and next action separately. Include unfamiliar wording and reversed comparisons.
3. Evaluate the whole visitor journey. Check homepage → Explore → project detail → Back/Forward → shared view, plus Work and photo navigation. Review desktop and narrow screens in both modes, keyboard use, reduced motion and layout containment.
4. Fix causes in the shared content/routing/view layer. Keep the quiet homepage and gallery intact. Prefer grounded authored evidence and purposeful interaction over additional interface controls or generic prose.
5. Retest each failure and its neighboring cases locally, including mocked provider disagreement. Run focused tests, typecheck/build and browser flows. Review screenshots for hierarchy and repetition.
6. Deliver a cohesive candidate and release checklist. Keep live findings distinct from candidate verification. Publication remains a separate owner instruction under AGENTS.md. After an authorized release, rerun the same live cases and correlate actual provider responses before closing the production audit.

Quality gates: no known wrong-subject or wrong-section results in the tested corpus; no unsupported factual claims; follow-ups preserve visible order and state; first useful answer and action are easy to find on mobile; citations remain available; no page overflow, uncaught page errors or stale-response overwrites.
