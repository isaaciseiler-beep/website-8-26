# Macintosh device frame

The active preview and default use the original Macintosh 128K isolated PNG, restored at the owner’s request. Its source is the owner’s `mac_128k_01-1351948931.jpg`; built-in ImageGen produced the front-facing reconstruction without the detached keyboard and mouse. It is an image-assisted reconstruction, not a measured factory photograph. The exact editing prompts are preserved below.

`MacintoshFrame` places the existing native Explore editor and results inside one scrolling display. `src/lib/macintoshModels.ts` owns source-image dimensions, screen quads, and silhouette clips. The projective transform preserves native text and hit testing; model changes keep the mounted exploration tree. All frames share the Guided Tour blue palette and Satoshi controls. CRTs add restrained barrel distortion and phosphor texture; LCDs keep a planar glass reflection.

The Computer selector retains optional model prototypes. The original Macintosh is selected for review. The G3 prototype was rejected as a final product photograph; the later Snow study is an inactive candidate under `docs/design/device-candidates/`. The G4 uses the owner’s supplied front-facing reference, edited to remove peripherals; see `docs/design/imac-g4-cutout.md`. The original Air uses a real transparent photograph. Credits and adaptation records are in `public/devices/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

Screen width is normalized across desktop models while respecting available width and viewport height. Hardware remains in document flow so the rest of the cabinet can be seen by scrolling. Inside the display, native text stays at its actual size, controls retain accessible names and touch targets, and the interface uses its own scrolling viewport. Smooth silhouette clips exclude residual extraction pixels against light and dark backgrounds.

## Image generation prompts

Initial edit:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: photorealistic website device frame, isolated chassis. Edit the attached photo of an original Apple Macintosh 128K (1984). Preserve this exact beige compact Macintosh 128K model: tall one-piece beige housing, original recessed black CRT surround, beveled front inset, single horizontal 3.5-inch floppy slot at lower right, tiny rainbow Apple badge at lower left, lightly aged authentic plastic surface. Remove the keyboard, mouse, all cables and shadows cast by those peripherals. Reconstruct the unobstructed bottom of the computer body faithfully. Make the front exactly straight-on with no perspective angle, centered symmetrically. Full chassis from top to bottom visible with a small amount of transparent breathing room, no white studio background: true alpha transparency outside computer. Replace the CRT display contents with flat pure black, retaining the physical dark rounded glass perimeter and bezel. Do not add text, controls, or other objects. High quality sharply detailed product photography, soft diffuse studio lighting, believable subtle plastic texture. Chassis fills almost the entire tall portrait output, approximately original 27.7 cm wide / 34.5 cm high front proportions. Crucial: no keyboard or mouse, no extra computer models, no graphics or writing on screen, no decorative background, no illustration look.

Geometry correction (correcting the initial prompt's width/depth confusion):

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Create the final web asset of the original Macintosh 128K chassis, front view, following Image 1 for the EXACT model and Image 2 for the clean unobstructed photography treatment. Critical correction: the chassis should be taller and narrower with real frontal dimensions 24.4 cm WIDE by 34.5 cm HIGH, so outer silhouette width / height is 0.707. Preserve original Macintosh 128K model only, original rainbow badge at lower left, original single 400K floppy slot at lower right, warm gently aged beige plastic, thick subtly rounded black CRT surround recessed in beveled housing. The inner luminous/usable screen opening must be aspect ratio 512:342 = 1.497, landscape screen. Screen contents flat pure black for later interactive HTML overlay. The top/sides/body proportions must be the Macintosh 128K, no later SE or Classic design changes. Center full chassis with small clean transparent margin. No keyboard, mouse, cable, extra shadows, background, added writing, UI, ornament, perspective angle, or other object. Photorealistic straight-on studio product photo with fine authentic material texture. Real alpha transparency outside computer.

Final cutout cleanup:

> Use case: background-extraction. Clean cutout of this exact Macintosh photograph. Preserve the computer and its shape, proportions, screen, materials, subtle shading, floppy drive, badge, front panel and all details exactly unchanged. Remove ALL isolated white, yellow, and pale stray pixels, blotches and haze outside the computer, especially above its top and along the right edge. Outside silhouette must be perfectly clean alpha transparency, not a white/black background and no checkerboard. No added shadows, peripherals, or objects. Keep the fine antialiased edges of the computer itself. Production-ready clean transparent PNG cutout. Do not alter or redesign the subject.

Residual extraction pixels beyond the silhouette are excluded by the image element's CSS clip. The PNG is preserved unchanged from the generated output.
